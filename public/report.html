Frequently Asked

About reporting

How long does it take to receive a ticket number?

Immediately upon submission. You'll receive a confirmation email with your ticket ID within 2 minutes.

Are all reports investigated?

Yes. Safety issues and complaints are prioritized. Suggestions are reviewed monthly by our planning team.

Can I remain anonymous?

Yes. Check the "Anonymous" option and we won't collect personal information. You'll still get a ticket number for follow-up.

What about safety issues?

Critical safety reports are escalated immediately. If life-threatening, call 100 (emergency) or visit the nearest help desk.

How do I track my report?

Use your ticket number at any help desk or call our support line. Status updates are sent via email if opted in.

Can I edit my report after submission?

Within 24 hours, yes. Contact support with your ticket number. After that, you can file a follow-up report.

Need immediate help?

For emergencies or urgent safety concerns, please contact authorities or visit the nearest help desk immediately.
