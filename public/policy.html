Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SG Metro Rail Corporation ("we," "us," "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (name, email, phone, fare payment details) and information collected automatically (location data, device ID, travel history for personalized recommendations).

  • Account Data: Name, email, phone, payment method
  • Journey Data: Entry/exit stations, timestamps, line used (for service optimization)
  • Device Data: IP address, browser type, app version (for technical support)
  • Preferences: Language, accessibility needs, communication preferences

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide metro services and process payments
  • Improve service quality through data analytics
  • Send service updates and real-time alerts
  • Detect and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Personalize user experience (only with opt-in consent)

Data Retention

Payment and account data are retained for 7 years (legal requirement). Journey history is retained for 2 years unless you request deletion. You can request data deletion anytime by contacting privacy@sgmetro.local.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@sgmetro.local with your ticket or account number.

Terms of Use

Last updated: July 2026

By using SG Metro services, you agree to these Terms. Please read them carefully.

Service Eligibility

  • You must be 18+ or have parental consent to purchase passes
  • You must comply with all station rules and local laws
  • Fraudulent payment methods or stolen credentials will result in immediate suspension

Ticket & Pass Terms

  • Passes are non-transferable and personal to the holder
  • Lost smart cards cannot be replaced without repurchase
  • Refunds only issued for unused passes within 7 days of purchase (see Refund Policy)
  • Monthly passes are automatically renewed unless canceled in the app

Liability Limitation

SG Metro is not responsible for personal injury, loss, or damage except where caused by our negligence. Maximum liability is limited to the fare paid for the journey.

Service Suspension

We reserve the right to suspend service to users who violate conduct rules, engage in fraud, or harass staff. Suspension is permanent unless appealed within 30 days.

Passenger Conduct Policy

Last updated: July 2026

All passengers must maintain respect for staff, fellow travelers, and metro property. Violations may result in fines or legal action.

Prohibited Behavior

  • Harassment, threats, or abusive language toward staff or passengers
  • Smoking, vaping, or consumption of alcohol
  • Littering or vandalism of stations or trains
  • Blocking doors or aisles; refusing to vacate priority seating
  • Playing loud music or noise disruption
  • Solicitation, busking, or unauthorized advertising
  • Photography or video recording without consent
  • Carrying dangerous, illegal, or hazardous materials

Dress Code

Metro staff have the right to deny boarding to passengers with inappropriate attire that violates public decency standards or local regulations.

Priority Seating

Priority seats are reserved for elderly passengers (60+), pregnant women, people with disabilities, and passengers with young children. Able-bodied passengers must vacate these seats.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 2026

SG Metro is committed to providing an accessible transit experience for all passengers, including those with disabilities.

Station Accessibility

  • All 42 stations feature wheelchair ramps and elevators
  • Tactile paving guides blind and low-vision passengers
  • Accessible restrooms at all major stations
  • Service animals are permitted at no charge
  • Trained assistance staff available 24/7

Train Accessibility

  • Wheelchair spaces in every train car with secure restraints
  • Priority seating for people with disabilities
  • Audio and visual announcements at every station
  • Braille signage and tactile markings throughout
  • Accessible door buttons reachable from wheelchair height

Assistance Services

Request assistance by pressing the help button in trains or speaking to station staff. We offer:

  • Personal assistance for boarding/alighting
  • Wheelchair storage and charging facilities
  • Mobility device repair support
  • Multilingual information (12+ languages)

Digital Accessibility

Our website and app are WCAG 2.1 AA compliant. Report accessibility issues to accessibility@sgmetro.local.

Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Unused Passes

  • Single Journey Tickets: No refund once issued (non-refundable)
  • Day Passes: Full refund if requested within 2 hours of purchase
  • Week Passes: Full refund within 7 days if unused
  • Monthly Passes: Pro-rated refund if canceled within first 7 days

Overcharge / Error Refunds

If you were charged incorrectly (e.g., double-charged), request a refund within 30 days with proof of payment. Processing takes 5–7 business days.

Service Failure Refunds

If service is unavailable for more than 30 minutes on your planned route, we offer:

  • Full refund for that day's pass
  • Credit toward future passes (if monthly/weekly)
  • Compensation credit of 50% of fare for minor delays (10–30 min)

How to Request a Refund

Submit a refund request within 60 days of purchase via the app, website, or at any help desk with your payment receipt. Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

Cookie & Tracking Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience. You can manage preferences in your browser or app settings.

Types of Cookies

  • Essential: Authenticate sessions, prevent fraud
  • Functional: Remember preferences, language, accessibility settings
  • Analytics: Understand how users navigate our services
  • Marketing: Personalize ads (only with consent)

Your Choices

Disable non-essential cookies anytime in your browser settings or app. Note: Disabling essential cookies may prevent login and service access.
